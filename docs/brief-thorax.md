# Module: thorax -> src/model/thorax.js (owner 'thorax' in INFO)
Covers: thoracic cavity 9.6 (left and right dissections, transverse sections at T4 and T8, isolated lungs and heart): lungs, heart, mediastinum, great vessels, nerves, lymph nodes, diaphragm, plus liver/reticulum visible caudal to the diaphragm. World coordinates.

## Key frame data
THORACIC (T1..T13 body centres, x ~0.31 .. -0.74), ribPath(i, side), costochondral(i, side), cartilagePath(i, side), RIB_HALFWIDTH, STERNUM, XIPHOID, sternumY(x), diaphragmAttachment(side), DIAPHRAGM_DOME, HEART (base, apex, center, radii), TRACHEA (you build the THORACIC trachea from TRACHEA[5] (inlet, x~0.32) to BIFURCATION; the neck worker builds TRACHEA[0..5]), TRACHEA_R, TRACHEAL_BRONCHUS_ORIGIN, ESOPHAGUS (you build from index 5 to the hiatus and a little beyond), intercostalX(i, y), ribXAtY(i, y).
The body worker builds ribs, vertebrae, sternum, skin and the skin projection lines; you build everything INSIDE. Write an internal helper for the inner thoracic wall (half-width at (x,y) from the rib paths minus ~1.5 cm, dorsal limit under the vertebral bodies, diaphragm surface) and clamp lung blob vertices with blob opts.deform so the lungs fill the pleural cavities without poking through ribs or diaphragm.

## What to build (thorax-owned refs; read INFO descriptions)
- diafragma: dome (kind 'diaphragm') from diaphragmAttachment both sides up to DIAPHRAGM_DOME (cupula ~6th-7th ICS), with oesophageal hiatus and caval foramen. higado (right, caudal to diaphragm, under caudal ribs) and reticulo (left cranioventral, against the diaphragm, a few cm from the heart) as abdominal context visible in the T8 cut; keep them inside the cage/abdomen.
- Lungs (kind 'lung'): left: pulmon_izq_craneal_cr (in front of the heart up to the inlet), pulmon_izq_craneal_cd (over the heart), pulmon_izq_caudal (large dorsocaudal on the diaphragm). Right: pulmon_dcho_craneal_cr (very cranial, passes left ventral to the trachea, ventilated by the tracheal bronchus), pulmon_dcho_craneal_cd, pulmon_dcho_medio, pulmon_dcho_caudal, pulmon_dcho_accesorio (medial, around the caudal vena cava). Small fissure gaps between lobes. Cardiac notches carved in the lungs + small highlight patch refs: escotadura_cardiaca_izq (larger, 3rd-4th ICS ventral) and escotadura_cardiaca_dcha (smaller). borde_basal_pulmon (thin line along the basal border: 6th costochondral junction -> 11th ICS dorsally, both lungs). surco_vena_cava (groove on the medial right lung). Right lung bigger; asymmetric cranial lobes as in the T4 section.
- Airways: traquea (thoracic part, ringed: slightly translucent tube with torus rings every ~2 cm), bronquio_traqueal (from TRACHEAL_BRONCHUS_ORIGIN to the right cranial lobe), bronquio_izq, bronquio_dcho.
- Heart (HEART.center; base dorsal ~3rd-5th rib; apex near sternum at 5th-6th cartilage; slightly left): ventriculo_dcho (cranial/right, wraps cranially, not reaching apex), ventriculo_izq (caudal/left, forms apex), atrio_dcho, atrio_izq, auricula_dcha (cranially directed), auricula_izq (on the left/auricular face caudal to the pulmonary trunk), grasa_coronaria (yellow fat in coronary + interventricular grooves), seno_coronario, huesos_corazon (two small ossa cordis in the aortic ring, visible in a cut), valves valvula_pulmonar, valvula_aortica, valvula_mitral, valvula_tricuspide (leaflet discs at the base, readable in a dorsal cut through the base: pulmonary cranial-left, aortic central, mitral caudal-left, tricuspid right), borde_ventricular_dcho (cranial border line), borde_ventricular_izq (caudal border line), cara_auricular (thin translucent patch on the left face), cara_atrial (right face). pericardio: translucent 'serosa' sac with sternopericardial attachment.
- Great vessels: tronco_pulmonar (from RV, cranial-left, dividing), arco_aortico (from LV, arching dorsocaudally), aorta_toracica (ventral-left of the vertebral bodies to the aortic hiatus), tronco_braquiocefalico (single arch branch in ruminants: subclavian arteries (context, ref a_subclavia) + bicarotid trunk -> a_carotida_comun thoracic origins; the neck worker continues them cranially from the inlet dorsolateral to the trachea), v_cava_craneal (to RA from cranial, formed at the inlet by jugulars + subclavians: connect near JUGULAR last point (0.36, 0.78, ±0.10)), v_cava_caudal (caval foramen -> RA, with right phrenic on it), v_azigos_izq (crosses the left face of the heart base to the coronary sinus), v_azigos_dcha (dorsal right, into the cranial cava), a_v_toracicas_internas (paired artery+vein along the inner sternum), pulmonary veins (context).
- Nerves: n_vago (left and right; left crosses the aortic arch), n_laringeo_recurrente (left loops around the aortic arch at the lig. arteriosum, right around the right subclavian, both running cranially along the trachea), tronco_vagosimpatico (short inlet segment), ganglio_cervicotoracico (stellate, at the inlet near the 1st rib, both sides), asa_subclavia (loop around the subclavian artery), n_frenico_izq (over the pericardium to the diaphragm), n_frenico_dcho (along the caudal cava), tronco_vagal_dorsal and tronco_vagal_ventral (on the oesophagus caudal to the heart to the hiatus). conducto_toracico (dorsal to the oesophagus cranially to the inlet).
- Lymph & thymus: nnll_mediastinicos_craneales, nnll_mediastinicos_medios (over the heart base dorsal to the arch), nnll_mediastinicos_caudales (large elongated node on the oesophagus caudal to the heart), nnll_traqueobronquiales (bifurcation + tracheal bronchus), timo (residual thoracic lobe, cranial mediastinum ventral).
- Mediastinum: mediastino_craneal, mediastino_medio, mediastino_dorsal, mediastino_caudal as very translucent serosa sheets near the median plane, selectable, not hiding organs.
