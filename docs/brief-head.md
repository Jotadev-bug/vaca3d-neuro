# Module: head -> src/model/head.js (owner 'head' in INFO, ~150 refs)
Covers: skull 9.1.A/B, conformation + superficial dissection of the head 9.2.A, sagittal/transverse cut content 9.3, small-ruminant head features 9.4.A.

## Head frame
Build everything in the LOCAL head frame:
```js
import { root } from '../geo.js';
import { makeHeadGroup } from '../frame.js';
const H = makeHeadGroup(); root.add(H);
place(ref, mesh, layer, H); both(ref, mesh, layer, H);   // both mirrors across head median plane z=0
```
Local axes: +x rostral (0 = occipital condyles / atlanto-occipital joint, 0.52 = muzzle tip), +y dorsal, +z left. Use SKULL, MANDIBLE, PHARYNX, HEAD_SKIN_EDGE from frame.js (orbit, facial tuberosity, infraorbital foramen, zygomatic arch, horn base and direction, TMJ, mandible angle, facial vascular notch, mental foramen, tooth rows, palate height...). Refine shapes freely but keep landmarks consistent since neck/body attach to them:
- larynx around PHARYNX.larynx (the neck worker starts the trachea at frame TRACHEA[0] in world coords, just caudal to it; use headToWorld to compare);
- jugular origin at local (0, -0.17, 0.085): v. maxilar + v. linguofacial must converge there (neck worker continues v_yugular_externa);
- head skin ('piel', kind 'skin') from muzzle to HEAD_SKIN_EDGE (local x=-0.02, y +0.13..-0.24, half-width 0.11). The body worker builds the neck skin from that ellipse. Do NOT build neck skin.

## What to build (all head-owned refs; read INFO descriptions)
- Skull as several selectable meshes: craneo (neurocranium incl. occipital/temporal/base), h_frontal (wide flat frontal forming the whole roof up to the nuchal line; cow poll), prot_intercornual (transverse ridge between horns, highest point), linea_nuca, prot_occipital, ap_estiloidea, ap_cornual (bony horn cores, pneumatized), fosa_temporal (concave fossa caudal to orbit under the cornual process), linea_temporal (ridge bounding it dorsally), ap_cigomatica_frontal, arco_cigomatico, h_cigomatico, bulla_lagrimal, orbit rim + eye (context, kind 'eye'), maxilla/nasal/lacrimal face bones (context), tub_facial bump, h_incisivo (edentulous premaxilla), escotadura_nasoincisiva; foramina as rings: ag_infraorbitario, canal_supraorbitario, ag_orbitorredondo, ag_oval; surco_supraorbitario groove line.
- Mandible: rama_mandibula + cuerpo_mandibula, ang_mandibula, escotadura_vasos, borde_interalveolar, borde_ventral_mandibula, ag_mentoniano. Teeth individually shaped: lower incisivos I1-I3 + caninos (incisiform), premolares P2-P4 and molares M1-M3 upper and lower (selenodont crowns); no upper incisors (almohadilla_dentaria pad). Hyoid: basihioides with lingual process, stylohyoid bones with ang_estilohioideo, attached toward ap_estiloidea.
- Horns: cuerno_base, cuerno_cuerpo, cuerno_vertice (keratin sheath segments, curving lateral then rostrodorsal), epiceras ring at the base, dermis_cuerno thin layer between sheath and bony core (visible in a cut).
- Paranasal sinuses as translucent 'sinus' volumes: seno_frontal_caudal (huge, whole caudal frontal, cornual diverticulum into the horn core + nuchal part), senos_frontales_rostrales (small, between orbits), seno_maxilar (over upper molars), seno_palatino (in hard palate, ventral to nasal cavity), seno_lagrimal (rostromedial to orbit), senos_conchales (inside conchae rostrally), seno_esfenoidal (small, skull base); openings ab_nasomaxilar, ab_maxilopalatina, com_seno_lagrimal as small rings/discs.
- Nasal cavity (visible with clip=sag:0): septo_nasal (thin median sheet, not reaching the floor caudally), concha_dorsal (long thin scroll), concha_ventral (thick scroll), concha_media (small caudal), conchas_etmoidales (cluster in the fundus), meato_dorsal/medio/ventral/comun as translucent 'airspace' slabs between them, organo_vomeronasal (paired tubes on the floor beside the septum).
- Mouth: labios, vestibulo_boca (airspace cheeks-teeth), cavidad_propia_boca, paladar_duro with rugosidades_palatinas (transverse serrated ridges) and rafe_palatino, papila_incisiva behind the pad, mucosa_boca, caruncula_sublingual, papilas_bucales (conical papillae on inner cheek pointing caudally). Tongue: lengua, torus_lingual (dorsal caudal hump), fosa_lingual (groove rostral to torus), tonsila_lingual (root), frenillo, papilas_mecanicas (cones/lenticular on torus), papilas_gustativas (vallate caudal to torus + fungiform dots). Soft palate (context).
- Pharynx/larynx: nasofaringe (airspace dorsal to soft palate), septo_faringeo, tonsila_faringea, orofaringe, tonsila_palatina + seno_tonsilar, laringofaringe, nnll_retrofaringeos_med (dorsal to pharynx), cartilago_tiroides (+ epiglottis, arytenoids, cricoid context), cavidad_laringe, lig_vocal, lig_vestibular.
- Superficial dissection (both sides): m_masetero (big, on ramus from zygomatic arch & facial tuberosity), m_buccinador, m_cigomatico, m_elevador_nasolabial, m_elevador_labio_sup, m_depresor_labio_sup, m_depresor_labio_inf, m_parotidoauricular. n_facial trunk emerging caudal to the ramus under the parotid; n_facial_bucal (horizontal across masseter), n_facial_marginal (along ventral border), n_auriculopalpebral (rostral to ear toward eyelids), n_cornual (from orbit along the temporal line to horn base), n_infraorbitario (from foramen fanning to nose/lip), n_mentoniano (from mental foramen to lower lip). v_maxilar (through parotid) + v_linguofacial (over mandibular gland) converging at the jugular origin; v_facial and a_facial crossing the mandible at the vascular notch and ascending the face. gl_parotida (caudal to masseter, ventral to ear), conducto_parotideo (ventrally around the masseter via the notch, then up its rostral border to the cheek), gl_mandibular (ventral to parotid at the angle into the intermandibular space). Lymph: nl_parotideo (rostroventral to ear, ventral to TMJ), nl_mandibular (rostral to the angle), nnll_retrofaringeos_lat (caudal, under the atlas wing, partly under parotid). Ear (context, skin layer).
- External: hocico (hairless muzzle, kind 'nose'), plano_nasolabial (dark glandular plate between nostrils and upper lip, fine pattern), nostrils, head skin.
- Small-ruminant features marked at the equivalent place on the cow head: filtro_nasal, gl_plano_nasal, gl_seno_infraorbitario (rostromedial to eye), gll_cornuales (caudomedial to horn base), barba (tuft of hair strands under the chin).

Note the head is tilted (HEAD.angle) so a world-x cut is oblique; that is acceptable.
