# Module: body -> src/model/body.js (owner 'body' in INFO)
Covers: skin of neck/trunk/limbs/tail/udder, trunk skeleton, thoracic limb/pelvis references, clinical points, parietal projections (9.5.A, 9.5.B). World coordinates.

## Key frame data
THORACIC, THORACIC_SPINE_H, THORACIC_SPINE_TILT (spinous tip = body centre + (-sin(tilt)*h, cos(tilt)*h)), LUMBAR, SACRAL, CAUDAL, VERTEBRA_LEN, ribPath(i, side), costochondral, cartilagePath, costalArch, RIB_HALFWIDTH, STERNUM, XIPHOID, sternumY, diaphragmAttachment, DIAPHRAGM_DOME, SCAPULA, ELBOW, FORELIMB, HINDLIMB, HEART, BIFURCATION, intercostalX(i,y), ribXAtY(i,y), CONUS_MEDULLARIS, LUMBOSACRAL_SPACE, CD1_CD2.
- HEAD_SKIN_EDGE + headToWorld: the head worker's skin ends at that ellipse in head-local coords (x=-0.02, y +0.13..-0.24, half-width 0.11). Your neck skin must start there and overlap 1-2 cm so there is no gap.
- NECK_SKIN sections + NECK_SKIN_P: use these exact sections for the neck skin (the neck worker places muscles/vessels inside). NECK_DEWLAP: the neck worker builds the dewlap sheet; keep the ventral neck consistent.

## What to build (body-owned refs; read INFO descriptions)
- Skin ('piel', kind 'skin'): continuous smooth shell: neck (from the head edge through NECK_SKIN) -> withers/shoulders -> barrel (deep, widening behind the ribs, ~0.38-0.42 half-width at flank/belly) -> rump (hook bones at coxal tubers, pin bones at ischial tubers) -> tail root; four legs (forelimbs along FORELIMB incl. elbow, carpus, fetlock, claws; hindlimbs along HINDLIMB with stifle, hock, claws), tail with switch, udder with four teats. Recognisably a Holstein-type dairy cow (deep chest, straight topline). Several overlapping lofts are fine; avoid visible seams. All skeleton inside the skin.
- Trunk skeleton (kind 'bone'): T1-T13 bodies + arches (context) with ap_espinosas_toracicas (spines T1-T12, tall at the withers, tilted caudally) and t13_anticlinal (T13 vertebra with vertical spine) as own ref; L1-L6 bodies (context), ap_espinosas_lumbares, ap_costales (long flat transverse processes); sacrum (5 fused, context) with cresta_sacra_mediana and cresta_sacra_intermedia; coxal bones (context) with tuberosidad_sacra (dorsomedial ilium border, ref) and visible coxal/ischial tubers; caudal vertebrae Cd1-Cd20 (vertebras_caudales) with ap_hemales (ventral processes of first caudals) and arcos_hemales (Cd2-Cd4 haemal arches); a_caudal_mediana and v_caudal_mediana (ventral, inside the haemal arches along the tail); spinal cord (context, kind 'nerve', in the canal from T1 caudally, tapering) ending at cono_medular (taper + marker at CONUS_MEDULLARIS); costillas (13 pairs from ribPath, one ref) + costal cartilages (kind 'cartilage': 9-13 registered as arco_costal, 1-8 context); esternon (7 sternebrae + xiphoid); scapula with spine and dorsal cartilage, humerus, radius/ulna with olecranon, carpus/metacarpus/phalanges, femur/patella/tibia/tarsus/metatarsus/phalanges (context, simplified but elegant).
- Palpable markers (both sides): punta_hombro (SCAPULA.shoulderPoint), ang_caudal_escapula (SCAPULA.caudalAngle), punta_codo (olecranon tip ~ELBOW); linea_cd1cd2 (small ring/line at CD1_CD2 dorsal).
- Clinical points with tiny needle glyphs (thin tube + marker): puncion_lumbosacra (LUMBOSACRAL_SPACE, dorsal midline L6-S1), epidural_lumbar (L1-L2 dorsal), epidural_caudal (Cd1-Cd2 dorsal).
- Parietal projections ON THE LEFT SKIN (kind 'projection' red / 'projection2' teal), mirrored to the right where applicable; lim_card_caudal_dcha, proy_escotadura_dcha, aus_tricuspide are RIGHT side only:
  - proy_diafragma: oblique line from the 6th costochondral junction ventrally, up and caudally to the 11th-12th rib dorsally.
  - linea_receso: from the 8th costochondral junction through the middle of the 11th rib to the 13th rib dorsally.
  - proy_borde_basal: 6th costochondral junction -> middle of the 9th rib -> 11th ICS dorsally.
  - area_pulmonar (translucent triangle) with lim_pulm_craneal (caudal border of triceps: caudal angle of scapula -> elbow) and lim_pulm_dorsal (horizontal below the epaxials from the scapula to the 11th ICS); caudoventral limit = proy_borde_basal.
  - area_cardiaca (translucent rectangle, 3rd-5th ICS from above the sternum to the shoulder-point line) with lim_card_dorsal, lim_card_ventral, lim_card_craneal, lim_card_caudal_izq (6th rib), lim_card_caudal_dcha (right, 5th rib).
  - proy_corazon (heart outline projected from HEART on the left wall) with proy_borde_vd (cranial ~3rd rib) and proy_borde_vi (caudal ~5th-6th rib).
  - proy_escotadura_izq (patch, 3rd-4th ICS ventral), proy_escotadura_dcha (right, smaller), proy_bifurcacion (marker at 4th-5th ICS mid height, BIFURCATION projected laterally).
  - Auscultation markers: aus_pulmonar (3rd ICS left ventral), aus_aortica (4th ICS left just below the shoulder line), aus_mitral (5th ICS left ventral), aus_tricuspide (right 3rd-4th ICS ventral).
  To place things on the skin, write a helper that projects a point laterally onto your analytic skin surface and offsets 4 mm outward; draw lines/areas by projecting many sample points.
